Your information is used only to:

AI Image Processing

When you generate an AI image, your uploaded photo and text prompt are securely transmitted to OpenAI for AI image generation. The generated image is returned to your device.

Image Storage

Generated images are stored only on your device if you choose to save them. We do not permanently store your uploaded photos or generated images on our own servers.
