Delete Your GlamFit Account

GlamFit uses Google Sign-In (OAuth) only for user authentication.

We do not create or store a separate user account, profile, or personal information on our own servers for authentication.

How to Remove Your Account

  1. Open the GlamFit app.
  2. Sign out of your Google account (if signed in).
  3. To completely remove GlamFit's access, visit your Google Account permissions:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Select GlamFit and choose Remove Access.

Data Deletion

GlamFit does not permanently store your authentication information on our servers. If you remove access from your Google Account, your connection with GlamFit is revoked.

If future versions of GlamFit allow cloud storage of user data, this page will be updated with account deletion instructions.
